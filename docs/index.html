Questions

Before you download.

How much does it cost?

NanaiNest is free to download on the App Store. Five spheres are free; further themed packs expand the collection.

Do I need an account?

No. You can start focusing straight away. Sign in only when you want to keep your collection in the cloud.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad, on iOS 16 or later.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. There's no tracking and no connection required — live radio is the one thing that needs to be online.

Do I keep the spheres and cards I hatch?

Yes. Every sphere you hatch and every card it forges is yours to keep, in your Collection Binder.

Wasn't this called AetherFocus?

It was. Same app, same team, new name — NanaiNest. Nothing you collected has gone anywhere.
